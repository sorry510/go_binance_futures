# Protocol

1. Exhaustively use Binance official Earn Wednesday articles through 2023-2024; additionally obtain only the last pre-2023 Earn Wednesday article as state warmup.
2. Extract only the table subsection labeled "Flexible Products" inside the article's Limited-Time/Trending Limited-Time Offers. Stop at the next product-section label such as Locked Products, Liquidity Farming, ETH/SOL Staking, Auto-Invest, Dual Investment, etc.
3. Exclude stablecoin/fiat bases. Do not mix Locked Products, staking, liquidity farming, Auto-Invest or Dual Investment.
4. For each article, form the set of ordinary tokens in Flexible Products. Event = token is in current set but absent from the immediately previous Earn Wednesday article's Flexible set.
5. A token that remains in consecutive weekly Flexible sets does not retrigger. If it disappears and later re-enters, that later re-entry is a new independent activation event.
6. Direction is preregistered LONG: a new limited-time Flexible Earn yield offer creates an incremental short-term incentive to hold/subscribe the token.
7. One article is one independent batch regardless of token count. Multiple tokens from the same article remain separate token-events but batch-equal analysis is mandatory if discovery is reached.
8. Before any post-event return is read, require same-name Binance USD-M history >=730 days at article publish time and prior 24 complete 1h QuoteVolume >=5M USDT.
9. Coverage gate: >=8 eligible events, >=8 unique tokens, >=8 independent article batches.
10. Failure freezes before returns. Do not use APR thresholds/increases, Locked Products, repeated standing weekly offers or other Earn product families to manufacture coverage.
