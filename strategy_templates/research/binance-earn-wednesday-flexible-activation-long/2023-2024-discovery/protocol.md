# Discovery Protocol

1. Use the 74 eligibility-passing activation events copied unchanged from the frozen feasibility audit.
2. Direction is LONG for every event. No token/APR/product-duration/category filtering is allowed.
3. Signal time is the official Earn Wednesday article publishDate. Entry is the first whole-hour Binance USD-M 1h open strictly after publishDate.
4. Measure raw log returns from entry open to the close after 1h, 4h and 12h. This is an early economic gate, not final TP8/SL6 performance.
5. Gate: event-weighted 12h mean >= +0.50%; batch-equal 12h mean >= +0.50%; >=60% of unique-token means positive; >=60% of independent weekly-batch means positive; both 2023 and 2024 event-weighted 12h means >0.
6. Batch-equal and annual checks are mandatory because many tokens share the same Wednesday timestamp and market environment.
7. Failure freezes the family: do not use APR thresholds/increases, select only Locked/Flexible subtypes after returns, change horizon/entry delay, delete weak tokens, or reverse to SHORT.
8. 2025+ articles and outcomes remain unread unless discovery passes.
9. Only if discovery passes may an exact replay be defined with leverage=4, TP8, SL6, fee=0.0005/side, slippage=5bps/side and single-position semantics.
