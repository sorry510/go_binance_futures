# Binance Earn Wednesday Flexible-Offer Activation → LONG — Discovery

Frozen universe: 74 production-eligible activation events from the feasibility audit, covering 38 unique tokens and 43 independent weekly Binance articles.

Direction is LONG. Entry is the first whole-hour Binance USD-M 1h open strictly after official article publishDate. Early-gate outcomes are raw log returns at 1h/4h/12h; they are not final TP8/SL6 performance.

Pre-registered gate:
- event-weighted 12h mean >= +0.50%
- batch-equal 12h mean >= +0.50%
- >=60% of unique-token means positive
- >=60% of independent weekly-batch means positive
- both 2023 and 2024 event-weighted 12h means >0

## Final result

- **74 events / 38 tokens / 43 batches**.
- 1h mean: **-0.1662%**.
- 4h mean: **-0.1791%**.
- 12h mean: **-0.7768%**.
- Batch-equal 12h: **-0.4518%**.
- Positive tokens: **15/38**.
- Positive batches: **17/43**.
- 2023: 44 events, 12h **-1.4357%**, win12 27.27%.
- 2024: 30 events, 12h **+0.1897%**, win12 50.00%.

The discovery gate fails strongly on economic magnitude, token breadth, batch breadth and 2023 sign. The modest positive 2024 result does not justify redesigning the family.

Decision: **freeze**. Do not introduce APR-change thresholds, switch to Locked Products, keep only 2024, delete weak tokens, change entry/horizon, reverse to SHORT, or inspect 2025+ for redesign. No exact TP8/SL6 replay and no DB import.
