# DeFiLlama Chain USDT+USDC Share

Hypothesis: a rising share of USDT+USDC within a chain's total USD-stablecoin supply indicates improving liquidity quality and should support the chain native token; a falling share indicates deterioration.

Before returns, coverage passed with 11 chain candidates and the rule was frozen: 7d share-change zero-cross, positive LONG / negative SHORT, next UTC day open, Binance USD-M history >=2y, signal-day QV >=5m, discovery=2023-2024, and no discovery 7d endpoint beyond 2024-12-31.

Canonical discovery: 754 events / 10 event-bearing symbols. Signed mean returns were 1d -0.1620%, 3d -0.1229%, 7d +0.2645%, with 5/10 symbols positive. 2023 7d mean was -0.2338%; 2024 was +0.7371%. Symbol-equal 7d mean was +0.4906%, but breadth remained only 50%.

The economic gate (+0.25%) narrowly passed, while the pre-registered >=60% positive-symbol breadth gate failed. 2025-2026 OOS was therefore not evaluated.

Decision: freeze the family. Do not remove weak symbols, alter the 7d window, reverse direction, or inspect OOS to rescue the hypothesis.
