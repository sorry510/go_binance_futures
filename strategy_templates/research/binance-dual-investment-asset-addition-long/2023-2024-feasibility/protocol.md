# Protocol

1. Use the already audited Binance official 2023-2024 title corpus.
2. Include only titles explicitly announcing Dual Investment support/addition for a new underlying asset.
3. Exclude recurring "New Dual Investment Products Launched" articles: those refresh target prices/settlement dates for already-supported underlyings and are not new asset-support events.
4. One official support/addition article is one independent policy batch regardless of token count.
5. Require at least 8 independent batches before any production eligibility or post-event return is inspected.
6. Direction would be preregistered LONG only if coverage passed.
7. Do not split weekly product refreshes into pseudo asset-addition events or merge stablecoin product refreshes to manufacture coverage.
