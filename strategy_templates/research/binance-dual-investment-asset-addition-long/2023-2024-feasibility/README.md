# Binance Dual Investment Asset Addition → LONG — Feasibility

The frozen hypothesis was that first-time support of a token as a Binance Dual Investment underlying could create incremental structured-product demand/access and therefore define a LONG catalyst.

The already-audited Binance official 2023-2024 title corpus contains 61 Dual Investment titles. Most are recurring "New Dual Investment Products Launched" updates that refresh target prices and settlement dates for already-supported assets; they are not asset-addition events.

Only **4 independent titles** explicitly announce new underlying support/addition:
- 2024-11: WBETH, BNSOL, SUI.
- 2024-09: TON, NOT, DOGS.
- 2024-05: USDC.
- 2023-12: FDUSD.

The preregistered minimum is 8 independent batches. Coverage therefore fails before USD-M eligibility, QuoteVolume, or any post-event return is inspected.

Decision: **coverage blocked / freeze**. Do not reinterpret weekly product refreshes as asset additions, split one article into pseudo-independent batches, or lower the batch gate.
