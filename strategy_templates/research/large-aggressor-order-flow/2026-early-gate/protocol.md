# Protocol
Use Binance Vision USD-M daily aggTrades. Previous full UTC day defines the 99th-percentile aggTrade notional and the causal 24-hour distribution of hourly large-aggressor signed flow. On the fixed signal day, first |z|>=3 triggers and |z|<1 rearms. Positive flow is LONG, negative flow SHORT; observe next-full-hour 1h/4h/12h returns.

The four-symbol download stalled on seven large BTC/ETH archives. The already-complete SOL/XRP fixed-date sample was used only as an early gate. Because it produced just five events and inconsistent 12h cross-symbol direction, expansion to BTC/ETH or older years was stopped. Do not lower q99 to q95 or 3 sigma to 2 sigma after seeing results.
