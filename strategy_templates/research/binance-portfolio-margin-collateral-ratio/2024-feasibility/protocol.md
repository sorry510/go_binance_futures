# Protocol
Official Binance Portfolio Margin collateral-ratio changes only.
Ratio increase was pre-registered LONG; decrease SHORT. Map the asset to the same-name USD-M perpetual; no ad-hoc symbol aliases.
Eligibility at event time: USD-M history >=730 days and prior 24 completed 1h QuoteVolume >=5M USDT.
A minimum of 8 eligible events was required before any return replay. 2024 produced 36 asset-events but only 7 eligible events. A pre-return search for 2023 did not yield a reproducible adjustment series, so discovery was not expanded further.
No returns were inspected and 2025/2026 were not consumed.
