# Protocol

1. Exhaustively scan Binance official CMS catalogId=49 across 2023-2024 for titles containing airdrop or token distribution terminology.
2. Keep only the first announcement in which Binance explicitly supports an external project's airdrop/distribution to holders of an already-existing crypto token.
3. The traded symbol is the holder/snapshot token, not the newly distributed token.
4. Direction is preregistered LONG because announced eligibility creates incremental holding demand/value before the snapshot or entitlement point.
5. Exclude Binance Launchpool, HODLer Airdrops, promotional campaigns, Learn & Earn, referral rewards, listing-only announcements, stablecoin/fiat distributions, and completion notices.
6. Deduplicate repeated/update announcements for the same holder-token/corporate action.
7. Before reading any post-announcement return, require the holder token's Binance USD-M contract to have >=730 days history at announcement time and prior 24 complete 1h QuoteVolume >=5M USDT.
8. Coverage gate is >=8 eligible event-symbols and >=8 unique holder tokens. Failure freezes without return inspection.
9. If coverage passes, discovery execution/forward-return protocol must be frozen before any return is read.
10. Do not merge Launchpool/HODLer rewards or cross-exchange airdrop support to manufacture sample size.
