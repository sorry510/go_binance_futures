# Binance Unplanned Transfer Suspension → SHORT — 2023-2024 Feasibility

Hypothesis: an already-occurring security/operational incident that removes token deposit or withdrawal access can reduce transfer utility and arbitrage access; if a mature USD-M contract is affected, direction is preregistered SHORT.

The official Binance Maintenance Updates catalogId=157 was exhaustively scanned for 2023-2024 suspension/pause titles. Planned network upgrades were excluded.

Only one qualifying announcement was found:
- 2023-07-05, Multichain incident: Binance announced cessation of deposits/withdrawals on specified bridge networks for POLS, ACH, BIFI, SUPER, AVA, SPELL, ALPACA and FARM.
- The announcement also states some Multichain-bridged deposits had already been suspended on 2023-05-24; therefore publication time is retained as the information timestamp rather than backdating an unobserved signal.

Production eligibility was evaluated before any post-event return:
- USD-M history >=730 days at publication.
- Prior 24 complete 1h QuoteVolume >=5M USDT.

## Final result

Raw coverage: 8 tokens, one common Multichain incident batch. **Eligible: 0/8.** Every token fails at the first production gate because the corresponding USD-M contract either did not exist or lacked two years of history at 2023-07-05.

Decision: feasibility blocked / freeze. No post-event returns were read. Do not lower the two-year requirement, backdate to the May operational suspension without a contemporaneous fixed-census announcement, or merge planned maintenance events.
