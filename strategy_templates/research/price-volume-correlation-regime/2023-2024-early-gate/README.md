# Price-Volume Correlation Regime
Rolling 24h Pearson correlation between hourly log price return and log QuoteVolume change. Positive zero-cross follows completed 4h return; negative zero-cross fades it. BTC/ETH/BNB/XRP 2023-2024 produced 3,938 events. Aggregate 12h signed mean +0.0265%, win 49.4%; 1h mean was negative.

Decision: freeze at early gate; statistical effect is well below the +0.10% expansion gate and execution-cost scale.
