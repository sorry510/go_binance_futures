# Protocol

Before any return inspection, require an official/public source that can reproduce historical CEX reserve or inflow observations across a discovery period and later untouched OOS.

Minimum requirement:
- historical dates covering at least 2023–2026,
- token-level balances or token-specific inflows/outflows, not only exchange-wide current totals,
- deterministic timestamps and auditable source semantics,
- no dependency on frontend-private caches or manual screenshots.

If the free/public source cannot satisfy these requirements, freeze the family without constructing a trading signal.
