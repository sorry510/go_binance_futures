# Binance P2P Asset Addition → LONG — 2023-2024 Feasibility

Hypothesis: first support of an ordinary crypto token on Binance P2P broadens fiat access/on-off-ramp utility and therefore defines a preregistered LONG event.

Coverage audit reused the already archived complete Binance CMS catalogId=49 2023-2024 title corpus (1,071 titles), avoiding another inconsistent/partial crawl.

- 29 titles mention Binance P2P.
- Most are merchant programs, fees, fiat-market additions, payment methods, regional trading-pair expansions, or platform changes.
- USDC, FDUSD, and TUSD additions are stablecoins and excluded by protocol.
- The only explicit first addition of an ordinary crypto token is **WLD**.
- "new trading pairs in selected fiat zones across Africa" is a regional corridor expansion and is explicitly not counted as a new token event.

## Final result

Raw semantic coverage: **1 event / 1 ordinary crypto token (WLD)**, below the preregistered >=8 events / >=8 tokens gate.

Production eligibility and post-event returns were not read.

Decision: **coverage blocked / freeze**. Do not split fiat corridors into separate events, include stablecoins, or merge Spot/Convert/Pay additions to manufacture coverage.
