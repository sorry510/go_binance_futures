# Protocol

1. Audit Binance official CMS 2023-2024 titles for Binance Pay-specific product announcements.
2. Qualifying event must explicitly add a named cryptocurrency as a Binance Pay spending/payment asset.
3. Generic Pay product changes, Pay ID migration, fees, Charity, merchant/payment-rail expansion, or regional availability do not qualify.
4. Direction would be preregistered LONG because explicit payment utility expands token usability.
5. Require at least 8 token-events / 8 unique tokens before production eligibility or returns may be read.
6. Coverage failure freezes the family; do not merge Earn/Convert/Card/Spot events to manufacture sample size.
