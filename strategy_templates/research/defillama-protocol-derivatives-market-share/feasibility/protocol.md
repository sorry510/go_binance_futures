# Frozen Feasibility Protocol

Before inspecting any Binance return, require a complete auditable daily history for both aggregate decentralized-derivatives volume and protocol-level derivatives volume.

If the public source does not expose both components, stop at data-access feasibility. Do not infer historical market share from current snapshots, charts, or a subset of protocols.

If data becomes available later, production eligibility remains dynamic Binance USD-M history >=2 years and prior-24h quote volume >=5M USDT. Any future discovery/OOS signal definition must be frozen before return inspection.
