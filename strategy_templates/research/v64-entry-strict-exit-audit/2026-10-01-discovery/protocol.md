# Protocol

1. Copy original v64 LONG/SHORT entry unchanged.
2. Replace only CLOSE_LONG/CLOSE_SHORT with ROI >= 8 || ROI <= -6.
3. Discovery: SOL/DOGE/LTC/AVAX/UNI/ZEC, 2023-2024 only.
4. Promotion: PF>=1.15, >=4/6 positive, frequency>=0.30/week, no clear 2023/2024 instability.
5. Failure => no KDJ period/J threshold changes, no trend-filter changes, no added filters.
6. OOS remains unread unless discovery passes.
