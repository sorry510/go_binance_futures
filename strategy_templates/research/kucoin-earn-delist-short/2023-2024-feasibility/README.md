# KuCoin Earn Delist -> Binance SHORT
Official KuCoin Delistings archive produced 24 Earn-delist articles in 2023-2024. Mechanical title/body parsing produced 34 token-events. Strict Binance production eligibility at announcement time (USD-M history >=730d and prior-24h QuoteVolume >=5M USDT) left only seven events: APE, GRT, GAL, DAR, BAND, IOST and ALGO.

Decision: freeze as insufficient discovery sample (<8). No post-event returns were inspected.
