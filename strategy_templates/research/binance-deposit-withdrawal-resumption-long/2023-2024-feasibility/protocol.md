# Protocol

1. Exhaustively scan Binance official Maintenance Updates catalogId=157 for 2023-2024.
2. Include only titles explicitly stating deposit/withdrawal/network service has resumed, reopened, or been restored.
3. Exclude planned upgrade notices saying service "will resume" automatically after completion; the signal must be a confirmed restoration announcement.
4. Direction, if coverage passes, is preregistered LONG because transfer utility/access has been restored.
5. Require at least 8 raw independent event-token observations / 8 unique tokens before any return is read.
6. Only after raw coverage passes would production eligibility apply: USD-M history >=730d and prior-24h QuoteVolume >=5M USDT.
7. Failure at either coverage stage freezes the family without post-event return evaluation.
