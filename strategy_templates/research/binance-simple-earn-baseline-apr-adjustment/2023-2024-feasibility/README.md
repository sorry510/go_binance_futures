# Binance Simple Earn Baseline APR Adjustment — 2023-2024 Feasibility

Hypothesis: durable baseline/ongoing Simple Earn APR increases for an already-supported ordinary crypto asset could be positive demand/carry events (LONG), while durable baseline APR decreases could be negative (SHORT).

Frozen event definition excluded limited-time promotions, Bonus Tiered APR campaigns, Super Earn, Locked-product launches/updates, first product additions, stablecoin/fiat promotions, Dual Investment, and generic marketing. A qualifying event required an unambiguous token-specific ongoing/base APR old→new direction.

Audit:
- 25 official Binance 2023-2024 APR/yield/rewards-policy candidate articles were fetched successfully.
- 15 are Locked-product launches/updates or offers.
- 5 are promotions or first-product support.
- 3 are Bonus Tiered APR promotions.
- 1 is an Earn Wednesday recurring promotion bundle.
- 1 is the 2023 Flexible Products APR/rewards-distribution policy revamp, but it does not provide token-specific numeric old→new baseline APR direction.

## Final result

**0 qualifying token-events / 0 tokens / 0 batches.**

Coverage gate fails before production eligibility. USD-M history/QV eligibility and all post-event market returns remain unread.

Decision: **feasibility blocked / freeze**. Do not reinterpret promotional APR as baseline APR, infer token-level numeric direction from the global policy change, include Locked products, add stablecoins/fiat offers, or lower the coverage gate.
