# Feasibility Protocol

1. Audit the complete archived Binance official 2023-2024 corpus for titles/body text related to Simple Earn APR, yield, rewards rate or reward-distribution policy.
2. Qualifying event must adjust the ongoing/base APR or rewards rate of an already-supported ordinary crypto asset and must expose an unambiguous direction (old->new numeric rate, or an explicit durable rate-policy change from which increase/decrease is mechanically determined).
3. Exclude limited-time/limited-quota offers, Bonus Tiered APR campaigns, Super Earn, BNSOL Super Stake, Locked-product launches, first asset additions, Dual Investment, stablecoin/fiat promotional offers and generic marketing.
4. Repeated campaign refreshes are not baseline-rate changes. A product-launch APR shown for a newly added product is not a rate adjustment.
5. Direction, if coverage passes, is preregistered APR increase -> LONG and APR decrease -> SHORT.
6. Coverage gate before any market eligibility or return: >=8 token-events, >=8 unique ordinary-crypto tokens and >=5 independent official article batches.
7. Only if event coverage passes, apply event-time same-name USD-M history >=730 days and prior 24 complete 1h QuoteVolume >=5M USDT; then freeze a separate discovery protocol before returns.
8. Failure freezes this family without market-return inspection. Do not reclassify promotional APR as baseline, split one article into pseudo-independent batches, add stablecoins, or lower coverage thresholds.
