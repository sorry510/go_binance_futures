# Binance Margin Dynamic Interest Rate — Feasibility

Hypothesis: asset-specific changes in Margin borrow rates could change leveraged spot borrowing costs and spill over into USD-M positioning.

A complete 2023-2024 Binance `Latest Binance News` title scan for margin/interest-rate wording produced one genuine Margin item: `Binance Margin Introduces Dynamic Interest Rate Updates` on 2023-02-20. The remaining matches were Futures funding-rate/leverage announcements already covered by other families.

The Margin item is a rule/mechanism announcement, not a historical sequence of asset-level rate-change events with public timestamps.

Decision: event-history/data-definition blocked before returns. Do not reinterpret Futures funding-rate announcements as Margin borrow-rate events.
