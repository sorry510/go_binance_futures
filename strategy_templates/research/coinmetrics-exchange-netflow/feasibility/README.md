# CoinMetrics Exchange Netflow Feasibility
CoinMetrics Community exposes FlowInExUSD, FlowOutExUSD and SplyExUSD for BTC and ETH, but not for XRP, ADA, LINK, BCH, LTC, DOGE, UNI or ZEC in the project 10-symbol research set.

Decision: data-feasibility blocked for cross-symbol strategy research. Do not create a BTC/ETH-only exception.
