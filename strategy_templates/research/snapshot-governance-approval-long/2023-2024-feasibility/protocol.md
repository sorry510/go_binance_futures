# Protocol

1. Reuse only the already archived complete 2023-2024 Snapshot proposal corpus mapped through DeFiLlama governanceID to mature, unambiguous Binance-token identities.
2. A qualifying proposal must have exactly two choices and exactly one clean positive label (For/Yes/Approve/Approved/Support or "For ...") and one clean negative label (Against/No/Reject/Rejected/Do not approve/No action/Oppose or "Against ...").
3. The positive option must have strictly greater final score than the negative option. Ties and ambiguous labels are excluded.
4. Signal time is proposal end, when the final result is knowable. Direction is preregistered LONG: explicit governance approval/consensus resolves the binary governance uncertainty in favor of the proposed action.
5. No title/body semantic filtering is allowed after this point; routine and material proposals remain in the same mechanically defined family.
6. Before any post-event return is read, require Binance USD-M history >=730 days at event time and prior 24 complete 1h QuoteVolume >=5M USDT.
7. Coverage requires >=8 eligible proposal-events, >=4 unique token symbols, and >=4 independent Snapshot spaces.
8. If coverage fails, freeze with no return inspection. If coverage passes, freeze discovery horizon/gates before loading post-event prices.
9. Do not use the two known negative-winning proposals, do not loosen label grammar, and do not use proposal creation time because the final approval is not yet known then.
