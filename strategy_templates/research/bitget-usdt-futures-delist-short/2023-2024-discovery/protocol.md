# Bitget USDT Futures Delist -> Binance USD-M SHORT — Frozen Discovery Protocol

1. Event source is the official Bitget Help Center "Trading pair delisting" section. Pages 16 through 24 form a continuous chronological archive spanning 2025-01 back through 2022-05, so 2023-2024 is fully bracketed without search-engine sample construction.
2. Candidate articles must explicitly announce futures/perpetual delisting. Spot-only, margin-only, postponement, and non-USDT coin-margined futures are excluded.
3. Corporate-action delists caused by token merge, migration, redenomination, or rebrand are excluded outcome-independently. Known exclusions include FET/OCEAN/AGIX, RNDR, GAL, FRONT, KLAY, TOMO, COCOS and the 10000AIDOGE denomination lifecycle.
4. Event timestamp is official Article datePublished. Direction is fixed SHORT. Entry is the next complete Binance USD-M 1h open strictly after publication.
5. Eligibility at publication: matching Binance USD-M exists, actual history >=2 years, and the 24 completed hours before the publication hour have QuoteVolume >=5m USDT.
6. Discovery is 2023-01-01 through 2024-12-31. OOS is not inspected unless discovery passes.
7. Frozen gate, fixed before reading returns:
   - >=8 eligible events, >=8 unique symbols, >=8 independent announcement batches;
   - event-weighted 12h signed mean >= +0.50%;
   - batch-equal 12h signed mean >= +0.50%;
   - >=60% of symbol-level 12h means positive;
   - >=60% of batch-level 12h means positive.
8. 2023 has only two eligible events, so no per-year positivity gate is imposed; year breakdown is descriptive only.
9. No post-hoc LONG reversal, corporate-action reinclusion, threshold change, delayed entry, or symbol filtering after returns are seen.
10. Exact TP8/SL6 with fees/slippage is permitted only after untouched OOS passes.
