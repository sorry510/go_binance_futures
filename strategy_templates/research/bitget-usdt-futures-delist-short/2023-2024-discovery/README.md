# Bitget USDT Futures Delist -> Binance USD-M SHORT — 2023–2024 Discovery

Bitget's official Help Center "Trading pair delisting" section provides a continuous chronological archive. Pages 16–24 bracket 2023–2024: page 22 reaches 2023-09 and page 24 is already in 2022. This allowed a complete official corpus rather than search-engine sample construction.

The archive produced 39 candidate futures/perpetual delist articles in 2023–2024. Corporate-action or denomination events (merge/rebrand/swap/redenomination) and non-USDT coin-margined contracts were excluded before returns. The normalized independent universe contained 52 token-events.

Strict Binance eligibility at Bitget publication reduced this to 20 events / 20 unique Binance USD-M symbols / 12 independent announcement batches: Binance contract exists, actual history >=2 years, and the prior 24 completed hours QuoteVolume >=5m USDT.

Frozen signal: SHORT at the next complete Binance 1h open strictly after Bitget Article datePublished.
