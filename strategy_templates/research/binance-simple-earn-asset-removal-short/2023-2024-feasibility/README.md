# Binance Simple Earn Asset Removal → SHORT — 2023-2024 Feasibility

Hypothesis: an explicit removal of an established token from Binance Simple Earn Flexible/Locked Products reduces platform utility and could create a negative event shock; direction would be preregistered SHORT only after a coverage gate.

Official Binance CMS catalogId=49 was exhaustively scanned for 2023-2024. There are 40 Simple Earn titles. Direct title matching found **0** removal/delist/cease/discontinue/close/suspend/redeem announcements.

A broad review of Update/Notice/Support/Migration-style titles produced one candidate: `Notice Regarding CYBER Simple Earn Flexible Products` (2023-09-07). Its body explains the CYBER redemption/liquidity incident and states that lower-liquidity tokens **may** be delisted from Simple Earn/Loans/Margin in the future. It does not announce an actual CYBER or other token Simple Earn removal.

## Final result

Explicit removal events: **0**. Coverage gate fails before production eligibility and before any post-event return is read.

Decision: coverage blocked / freeze. Do not treat hypothetical future-removal language as an event, search individual token pages to manufacture events, or merge unrelated Spot/Margin/Loan delistings.
