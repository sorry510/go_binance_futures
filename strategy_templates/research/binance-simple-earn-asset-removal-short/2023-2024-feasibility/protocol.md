# Protocol

1. Exhaustively scan Binance official CMS catalogId=49 titles for 2023-2024.
2. First pass: titles containing Simple Earn plus direct removal terms (remove/removal/delist/cease/discontinue/close/suspend/redeem).
3. Second pass: all Simple Earn titles, with broad review terms (update/notice/support/migration/redemption/terminate) to catch generic notices.
4. Read article body only for broad-review candidates; inclusion requires an explicit contemporaneous removal/delisting/discontinuation of named token support from Simple Earn.
5. Statements that tokens may be removed in the future are not events.
6. Before any returns, require >=8 eligible events / >=8 unique tokens after >=730d USD-M history and prior-24h QV >=5M.
7. If title/body census yields fewer than 8 explicit events, freeze without market-return evaluation.
