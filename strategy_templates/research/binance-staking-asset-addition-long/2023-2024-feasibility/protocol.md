# Protocol

1. Reuse the archived complete Binance official CMS catalogId=49 title corpus for 2023-2024.
2. Keep only first token-level announcements explicitly adding/launching staking support on legacy Binance Staking, Locked Staking, or DeFi Staking.
3. Direction is preregistered LONG because staking support adds yield utility and can increase holding demand.
4. Exclude Simple Earn (already separate family), ETH Staking generic/product updates, Launchpool, APR-only promotions for already-supported assets, removals, and generic platform changes.
5. Deduplicate by token; later tenor/APR/product variants are not new events.
6. Before any post-event return is read, require Binance USD-M history >=730 days at announcement time and prior 24 complete 1h QuoteVolume >=5M USDT.
7. Coverage gate: >=8 eligible events and >=8 unique tokens. Failure freezes without return inspection.
8. Do not merge Simple Earn additions or promotional offers to manufacture coverage.
