# Protocol

1. Audit all 2023-2024 official Binance titles referring to Copy Trading, Lead Trader, or Copy Trader.
2. A qualifying event must explicitly add or remove a named already-existing USD-M perpetual contract from Futures Copy Trading symbol support.
3. Product launch, global leverage/cooling/copy-amount rules, trader programs, campaigns and Spot Copy Trading are not token-specific support events.
4. Coverage gate before any market data: >=8 token-events, >=8 unique symbols and >=5 independent official batches.
5. Failure freezes this family without USD-M eligibility or returns. Do not reinterpret global feature changes as symbol events.
