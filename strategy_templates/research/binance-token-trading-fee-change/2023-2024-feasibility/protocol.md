# Protocol

1. Start from the complete Binance Latest News 2023-2024 title census.
2. Keep only Spot/Margin fee changes whose article body explicitly identifies ordinary crypto trading pairs or base tokens.
3. Fee relief start/expansion is preregistered LONG; restoration/increase of standard fees is SHORT.
4. Exclude P2P/fiat-only, stablecoin-only, global VIP-tier, market-wide Futures/COIN-M, Pool, BFUSD purchase, referral/commission, and articles whose ordinary-token universe cannot be reconstructed from the article itself.
5. One article/policy change is one independent batch regardless of how many pairs/tokens it lists.
6. Mixed-direction articles are excluded unless a token-level direction is unambiguous from the body.
7. Before returns, each batch must contain at least one token with Binance USD-M history >=730 days at announcement time and prior-24h QuoteVolume >=5M.
8. Require >=8 production-eligible independent batches. If fewer, freeze without reading returns.
9. Do not split one policy batch into token-events to satisfy the batch gate.
