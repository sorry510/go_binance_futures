# Binance Token Trading-Fee Change — 2023-2024 Feasibility

Hypothesis: explicit token-level Spot/Margin fee relief start or expansion may increase trading accessibility/liquidity (LONG); restoration or increase of fees may reduce it (SHORT).

A complete 2023-2024 Binance Latest News fee-title audit was reduced under a preregistered definition to **8 independent ordinary-crypto policy batches**. One article counts as one batch regardless of how many token pairs it lists. P2P/fiat, stablecoin-only, global VIP, market-wide Futures/COIN-M, and unreconstructable broad-pair universes are excluded.

The coverage gate requires all 8 raw batches to remain production-eligible, because the minimum is 8 independent batches.

The BETH zero-maker-fee batch (2023-04-10) fails immediately: Binance Vision has no `BETHUSDT` USD-M 1h archive in either the event month (2023-04) or the two-year cutoff month (2021-04), both returning 404. Therefore at most 7 independent batches can satisfy production eligibility.

## Decision

**Coverage blocked / freeze before returns.** No post-event return was read. Do not split one policy article into many pseudo-independent token events, lower the eight-batch gate, merge fiat/P2P/global promotions, or reinterpret later pair listings to rescue coverage.
