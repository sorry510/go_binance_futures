# Wikipedia Attention Shock Feasibility
English Wikipedia pageview data were accessible for most assets, but canonical asset-page mapping was not stable enough across the 10-symbol universe. BNB exact-token pageviews were only single digits/day in the 2023 audit window, while the XRP Ledger mapping had no retrievable 2023 pageview history. Network-vs-token page choices would therefore introduce symbol-specific semantic bias.

Decision: data-quality blocked. Do not cherry-pick alternative pages per symbol after looking at returns.
