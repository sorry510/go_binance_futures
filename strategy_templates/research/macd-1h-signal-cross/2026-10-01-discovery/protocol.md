# Protocol

1. Freeze standard 1h MACD(12,26,9) strategy before returns.
2. LONG: completed DIF[1] > DEA[1] and DIF[2] <= DEA[2]; SHORT mirrors.
3. Current price must break trigger-hour high/low.
4. CLOSE_LONG and CLOSE_SHORT are exactly ROI >= 8 || ROI <= -6.
5. Discovery only: SOL/DOGE/LTC/AVAX/UNI/ZEC, 2023-01-01 to 2025-01-01.
6. Promotion requires PF>=1.15, >=4/6 positive, frequency>=0.30/week, no clear year instability.
7. Failure => no MACD period scan, no extra filters, no reversal.
8. 2025 OOS1 and 2026 OOS2 remain unread unless prior stage passes.
