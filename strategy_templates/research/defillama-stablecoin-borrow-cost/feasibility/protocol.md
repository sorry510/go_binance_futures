# Frozen Feasibility Protocol

Before inspecting returns, require historical borrow-side APY or utilization/borrow amount for a mechanically reconstructable stablecoin lending universe spanning multiple mature chains.

The metric must represent borrower cost or leverage demand directly. Deposit APY alone is not an acceptable substitute.

Historical universe membership must be reconstructable without conditioning on pools that survive in the current catalog. If either borrow-side history or historical universe reconstruction is unavailable, stop before return inspection.
